Mission brief

How much do you remember?

This is a quick refresher, not a grammar lecture. Use am, is, are to talk about people, places, jobs, preferences, and personal information.

Challenge: Don't just click. When you see a sentence, read it aloud. When you see a question, say the answer aloud too.
Mission 1 · 5 rounds

Choose the BE

Choose am, is, or are. Think about the subject first.

Mission 2 · 5 rounds

Fix It

One version is correct. Choose it, then read it aloud.

Mission 3 · 5 rounds

Ask & Answer

Choose the correct question starter. Then say the complete question and short answer aloud.

Mission 4 · production

Quick Profile

Write short answers. Then read your profile aloud and tell a partner three things without looking.

Useful patterns:
My name is… · I am… · I'm from… · My favorite ___ is… · My hobbies are…
Speaking challenge: Close the profile after writing. Tell your partner three facts about yourself. Then ask: Are you…? or Is your…?
